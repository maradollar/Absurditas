Music attribution also appears during the closing scene and in the exported file metadata. Keep these credits in the YouTube description or wherever the film is published.

**Short transitions and interface effects:** whoosh-short, notification, key-press, error and click-soft from the HyperFrames 0.9.12 bundled sound library. The bundle's CREDITS.md identifies these as Pixabay sounds under https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/ . Used only as part of this video, not redistributed as standalone sound files. No attribution is required by that license; source is recorded for transparency.

**Vehicle and tyre foley:** original Absurditas sound design already used in the earlier cartoon preview, placed afresh against the Mark narration. These isolated local effects contain no uploaded speech.

**Additional original sound design:** deterministic synthesized cloth movement, cup contact, liquid, footsteps, train rumble, door movement, train warning tones, football impact and crowd texture. These are designed effects, not presented as field recordings. Their actual cue times are recorded in production/audio-cues.json.

**Narration:** the user's newly supplied Mark / ElevenLabs MP3. The complete performance is used at its original speed and pitch, with level normalization and padding after its end. The separate source recording and audio stems are not included in the public source branch.

**Fonts:** Manrope, SIL Open Font License; file and license supplied in assets/fonts/.
